# SESSION 2026-10-15 W (Claude chat): Leaderboard and Review showed only a flicker of the Packages page

> Repo is PUBLIC. Follows `docs/SESSION_2026-10-15_V.md`. `[~]` written, NOT compiled, not seen on a device.

## Owner report
Tapping Leaderboard or Review only flickered the Packages screen and showed nothing, "like something is underneath". Owner question: why is the competition page not independent, why is Packages showing?

## Root cause (code read, strong evidence; not yet confirmed on the device)
- `scripts/patch_c4_trophy_refresh.py` (read) mounts the hub in `MainActivity.kt` as an OVERLAY inside the Scaffold content: `CompetitionsHubScreen(onBack, modifier = Modifier.fillMaxSize().background(WisdomNavy).zIndex(17f))`. So the top bar and the bottom tabs belong to MainActivity, and the Packages tab stays composed underneath.
- `CompetitionsHubScreen` applied that `modifier` ONLY to the contest list. The leaderboard, the review and (in the first test) the exam were drawn with a plain `Modifier.fillMaxSize()`, with no background and no zIndex, so other layers of MainActivity were drawn on top of them. The student saw the Packages page with a flicker. This is also what happened in the first exam test (Start led to the Packages page).

## Fix
`CompetitionsHubScreen.kt`: the list is always the base (it keeps the overlay modifier); the exam, the rules page, the leaderboard and the review are each shown through `CompetitionFullScreen` (a dialog = its own window above everything, independent of the layering of MainActivity). `CompetitionLeaderboardScreen.kt` and `CompetitionReviewScreen.kt` were not changed: each already draws its own `AtmosphereBackground`, has its own Back button and a `BackHandler`. No MainActivity change.
