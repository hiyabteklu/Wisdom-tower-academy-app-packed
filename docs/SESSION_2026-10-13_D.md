  - Root cause (read from code, hypothesis for the exact look on the phone): each orb was a solid circle with `Modifier.blur(80.dp / 70.dp)`. Compose clips a blur to the layer's own square box by default, so the glow is cut flat at the box edge (the hard rectangles in the owner's screenshots); on Android 11 and older `blur` does nothing, so the orb was a hard-edged circle.
  - Fix: no blur. Two radial gradients drawn with `drawBehind` that fall off smoothly to fully transparent at their own edge, clipped to the screen area. Same colours and centres (cyan 130dp/70dp, purple 280dp/240dp from the top-left corner). Peak strength 0.11 (cyan) and 0.08 (purple), radii 220dp and 190dp: chosen to match the old blurred strength (the old peak after blurring was about 0.11 and 0.08 by my estimate, labelled hypothesis).
  - Signature unchanged: `AtmosphereBackground(modifier)`; all 25 callers untouched. Added imports: `clipToBounds`, `drawBehind`, `Offset`, `DrawScope`. Old imports (`blur`, `offset`, `size`, `CircleShape`) are now unused and left in on purpose (unused imports do not break the build).
